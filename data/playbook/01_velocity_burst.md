# Velocity Burst (Account Draining)

## Typology
A compromised account sends many payments in a few minutes, usually to several payees it has never paid before. Amounts are often kept just under review thresholds (for example 9,000-9,900 when the threshold is 10,000), a technique known as structuring.

## Red flags
- 6 or more outgoing payments within 5 minutes.
- Most payees are new to the account and were opened recently with minimal KYC.
- A device that has never been seen on the account.
- The account's normal behaviour is a handful of payments per day.

## Investigation steps
1. Compare the burst to the account's 30-day baseline (payments per day, usual device).
2. Check the payees: account age, KYC level, and whether they receive money from other unrelated accounts at the same time.
3. Check whether payees forward the funds onward (use money-flow tracing).

## Typical action
Freeze the paying account to stop further losses and contact the customer through a verified channel. This requires human approval.
